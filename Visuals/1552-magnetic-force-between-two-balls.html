Magnetic Force Between Two Balls / visualized

Sort the baskets, then stop thinking about them. The search space is every candidate minimum gap from 1 to max − min. Only placeable(gap) ever touches the array: it walks left to right and drops a ball at the first basket at least gap past the previous one. Bigger gaps can only fit fewer balls, so placeable is monotone non-increasing — and that is the licence to binary search the answer instead of the data.

H, not G — the branch that flips

410 and 1482 minimise a maximum; this one maximises a minimum, so every comparison and every assignment reverses. Feasible here means “this gap is small enough to still fit m balls”, so a feasible mid is a lower bound on the answer: keep it with lo = mid and throw away the small gaps below. Infeasible means the gap is too greedy: hi = mid - 1. And because lo = mid does not move lo past mid, the midpoint must round up — mid = lo + (hi - lo + 1) / 2 — or a two-value range spins forever. That off-by-one is the single most common way this family is written wrong.

Choosing lo and hi — the step people get wrong

lo = 1: baskets are distinct, so a gap of 1 always fits every ball — a guaranteed feasible floor, which is what an H-shape search needs to have in hand before it starts. hi = max − min: the two outermost baskets are the widest separation that exists, so no gap beyond it can fit even two balls.

  • Set hi too small and the true answer sits outside the range — the search returns the largest gap it happened to look at, not the largest that works. Silently wrong.
  • Overshooting hi is harmless: extra iterations, same answer. When unsure, overshoot.
  • The tighter bound (max − min) / (m − 1) is also valid and converges faster, but it is an optimisation — get the loose one right first.

Sorting is not decoration. The greedy inside the predicate only works because “the next basket far enough along” means the next one in coordinate order; placing the leftmost ball at position[0] is optimal because pushing it right can never help a gap.

O(n log n) sort + O(n · log(max − min)) search · O(1) extra space  ·  maximise the minimum: feasible keeps mid (lo = mid) and the midpoint rounds up.
