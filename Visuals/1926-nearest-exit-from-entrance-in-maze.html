Nearest Exit from Entrance in Maze / visualized

The BFS here is the same machine as 1091 — levels are distances, mark on push. What is new is the exit predicate: a border cell is an exit unless it is the entrance. Get that wrong and every maze whose entrance sits on the border answers 0. The fix that needs no guard at all is to test the neighbour as you step onto it, after the entrance has already been sealed.
