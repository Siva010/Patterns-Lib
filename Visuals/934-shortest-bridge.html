994 hands you the rotten oranges, 542 hands you the zeros, 1162 hands you the land. Here nobody hands you anything: you must run a whole first traversal — a DFS, exactly the one in LC 200 — just to discover the seed set, and only then does the machine from the other three pages apply. Phase one paints one island; phase two floods outward from every cell of it at once. The bridge is the water flipped, so the answer is levels minus one — level 1 is the island itself and flips nothing.
