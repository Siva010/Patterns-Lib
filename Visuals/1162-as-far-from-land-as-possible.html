As Far from Land as Possible / visualized

Seed every land cell before the first pop and the water fills in level by level, each cell reached at exactly its distance to the nearest land. But 1162 never asks for that field. The answer is the depth of the last level — a single counter — so the whole matrix is scaffolding that gets thrown away at return. Run the identical machine, keep the array, and you have LeetCode 542 instead; the difference between the two problems is one line.

O(m·n) time and space  ·  the level numbers on the board are drawn for the reader — the program stores none of them. It keeps one int, and because the seeding round is the land itself, that counter starts at -1, not 0. All land or all water and no water cell has any land to be far from: -1, which is the same sentinel for two opposite grids.
